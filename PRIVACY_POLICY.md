# Privacy Policy for Floating Drawing Pad

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Floating Drawing Pad ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a free tool application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information when you use our Service.

## Information Collection and Use
We do NOT collect, store, process, or share any personal data, usage data, or sensitive device information. All features of the application, including screen drawing and floating overlay, operate entirely locally on your device.

## Permissions Used
The app requests the following system permissions solely to perform its primary function (drawing over other apps):
- **SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW**: Used strictly to draw the floating toolbar and canvas on top of screen content.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE**: Used strictly to keep the overlay active while you use other apps.

None of these permissions are used to collect user data, monitor activity, or capture keylogs/screen recordings.

## Third-Party Services
The application does not use any third-party services, analytics, ad networks, or external SDKs that may collect information used to identify you.

## Security
We value your trust in using our app. Since we do not collect or transmit any data over the internet, your information remains entirely secure on your personal device.
